## Naming assessment and limits

The descriptive portfolio label identifies the subject and its Lang Systems source without launching a new standalone commercial brand. This is a presentation recommendation, **not legal clearance or a claim of registered rights**.

Names and artwork require different checks. Copyright protects original expression such as software and artwork; names themselves generally are not protected by copyright. [Queensland Government copyright guidance](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/running-business/risk/ip/types/copyright/defined). Trade marks identify the source of goods/services; descriptive wording may lack distinctiveness and confusingly similar marks matter. [IP Australia guidance](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/what-are-trade-marks/what-cant-be-a-trade-mark).

The 20 October web review found established uses of the broader phrase, including [Renishaw Licence Manager](https://www.renishaw.com/cmmsupport/en/uccserver-offline-licence-activation--34669) and [AWS License Manager](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/license-manager/latest/userguide/). The phrase [LM Signature also appears on an unrelated retailer's website](https://www.lmsignatureshop.com/). These are discoverability/confusion signals, not findings of infringement or Australian registration status. No visual-similarity determination was made about the approved monogram.

An exact-name web search did not surface a matching product for the full proposed portfolio title, but results were noisy and this does not prove availability. No completed trade mark register clearance is claimed. Before any commercial restart, review the actual name and artwork, similar words/images, relevant goods/services and target markets using [Australian Trade Mark Search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search) and obtain qualified advice where needed. IP Australia's [search guidance](https://ipfirstresponse.ipaustralia.gov.au/options/check-if-someone-else-already-owns-word-logo-or-idea-you-want) explicitly warns that absence of results does not establish safety. Preserve artwork provenance and establish redistribution terms separately.

## Restart checklist

1. Confirm a new owner request, intended audience, funded scope and whether the work is a portfolio change or a production candidate.
2. Review current source revisions, prior evidence and outstanding issues; rerun checks rather than treating historical results as current.
3. Reconfirm the name, logo provenance and distribution terms before commercial positioning or distribution.
4. Keep the public fictional demo separate from private backend/appliance engineering. Any future production candidate needs its own security, recovery, infrastructure, identity and operational acceptance, plus owner approval before real customer data.
5. Reassess browser behaviour, responsive/accessibility checks, sample journeys and service-worker updates before changing the published demo. Record what was actually tested and what remains unverified.
