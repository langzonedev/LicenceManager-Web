# Licence Manager product identity

Greg approved the LM Signature for prototype use on 18 October 2026. The angular L/M and two key notches identify the product; they do not replace the separate Lang Systems company identity. The product name and identity remain provisional for any future commercial launch.

The SVG artwork was constructed as editable geometric paths from the custom AI-assisted concept approved in the product conversation. No stock logo or third-party logo paths were incorporated. Concept approval and original construction are not trade mark clearance or a guarantee of exclusive rights; review the name and visual similarity before commercial release. No registration is claimed.

- `licence-manager-mark.svg`: navy/cyan master for light backgrounds.
- `licence-manager-mark-dark.svg`: white/cyan for charcoal backgrounds.
- `licence-manager-mark-mono.svg`: single-colour navy master.
- `icon.svg`: charcoal application tile with safe inset mark.
- `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png`: PNG exports of the icon for PWA installation.

Keep the aspect ratio, both key notches and all negative spaces. Keep at least 12.5% clear space around the standalone mark. Use the mark with adjacent live product text in application chrome; no external font is needed. Empty alternative text is correct when the adjacent text already says Licence Manager. SVGs contain no scripts, fonts, external references or raster images.
